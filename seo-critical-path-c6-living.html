Reading the map: the ring is the category's existing grade, nothing is recalculated. Moving dots mean work is flowing; on an at-risk track they slow down, and on a failing track they stop entirely and the track pulses red. The flywheel spins while its four stations are healthy, slows when one is at risk, and jams when one is behind, because a jam anywhere on the wheel stalls the whole cycle. Migrations is an optional bypass: the dev team increasingly covers it, so it never fails the main line.
