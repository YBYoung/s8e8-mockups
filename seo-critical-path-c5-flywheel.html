Reading the map: the ring is the category's existing grade, nothing is recalculated. The Migrations bypass between Onboarding and Strategy is optional: skipping it is normally fine since the dev team increasingly handles migrations themselves, so a slow bypass never fails the main line. The circle on the right is the flywheel: content and service pages feed Audits and Listing Reviews, whose findings become On-page and Support work, which spins the wheel again. A jam anywhere on the wheel slows the whole cycle, so a breach there flags every station on the loop. A dashed card is riding a failing track: the risk is starvation, not lateness.
