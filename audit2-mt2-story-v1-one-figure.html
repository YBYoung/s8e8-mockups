How to read the words

Dental searches overall: how much people search for dentists in general. Market Trends measures it with 5 common dental searches: invisalign, root canal, wisdom teeth removal, veneers and teeth whitening.

Rising: searches for the topic are growing clearly faster than dental searches overall. Steady: moving about the same as dental searches overall. Fading: clearly slower. Emerging: small but growing fast.

Clearly means more than 15 in every 100 ahead or behind, comparing July to September with the same three months a year earlier. Example: if dental searches overall stay flat and a topic grows 20 in every 100, it is 20 in every 100 ahead, so it is Rising.

October is not over yet, so it is left out.

02 · Why Steady, when the lines doubled

Google's scores for every dental search about doubled, so Market Trends looks past the jump.

From 2024 to 2026 Google's scores roughly doubled for every dental search this check read, including the 5 common dental searches behind dental searches overall. Google Trends alone can't tell whether more patients searched or Google changed its scale. So a topic is never called Rising just for that jump.

Compared with dental searches overall, teeth whitening barely moved until this spring. Dental implants grew well past dental searches overall in 2025, and this year it kept growing a little faster than them.

05 · One-off jumps

Teeth whitening had one big April, then came back down.

In April 2026 whitening searches reached more than twice their usual level, the busiest month in five years. By September they were back to about 35 on Google's 0 to 100, near the usual level of 30. April 2025 and April 2024 were ordinary, so this was a one-off, not a season.

06 · Seasons

Neither topic has a season to plan around.

Laid over each other, the last three full years don't agree. In 2023 and 2024 every month of both topics stayed within about 10 in every 100 of an average month; 2025 climbed to a July high. Whitening's April 2026 was a one-off, as chapter 05 showed. Either topic can be written any month.

A season needs the same busiest month in at least 2 of the last 3 years, and that month must run at least 15 in every 100 above an average month.
