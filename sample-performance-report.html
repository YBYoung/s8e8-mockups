Brief Summary

April through June 2026 brought meaningful growth in search visibility and website traffic, both Quarter-over-Quarter and Year-over-Year, which tells a clear story about your practice building real presence in Google search. The quarter's most significant concern, however, is a sharp drop in key events, which are actions taken on your website, which fell to 6 from 53 the previous quarter. Due to the abrupt nature of this drop it does seem to be a tracking issue, not an issue of users failing to engage with the website.

Website Traffic

Your website drew 1,878 sessions this quarter, up 29% Quarter-over-Quarter and a dramatic 352% Year-over-Year compared to 415 sessions in the same period last year.

New users followed the same trajectory: 1,468 this quarter versus 264 a year ago, a 456% increase Year-over-Year. More people found your site, and more of them were first-time visitors - a pattern that points to growing reach rather than just repeat traffic. What stands out as a concern is what happened after those visitors arrived. Key events totaled just 6 this quarter, down 89% Quarter-over-Quarter from 53 last period and down 88% Year-over-Year from 48. With more visitors coming to the site than ever, the gap between traffic volume and actions taken on your website is worth investigating - whether that reflects a tracking configuration change, a form or button issue, or something else on the site itself.

Search Visibility

Search performance this quarter is genuinely strong. Clicks from Google search reached 1,552, up 30% Quarter-over-Quarter and up 528% Year-over-Year from just 247 clicks in the same period last year.

Search impressions came in at 288,683, up 432% Year-over-Year, though essentially flat compared to the previous quarter. The contrast between those two comparisons tells an important story: your visibility in search has expanded dramatically over the past year and has now reached a level of stability, while the continued growth in clicks suggests the searches you are appearing for are increasingly relevant to what patients are actually looking for. A year ago, search was contributing very little to your practice's discoverability; today it is one of the clearest signs of progress we track.

The pages driving that traffic tell an equally clear story. Your top five landing pages by new visitors this quarter were:

  1. A Parent's Guide to Frenectomies: What to Expect (813 new users)
  2. White Dental Crowns for Children, Explained (222 new users)
  3. Homepage (183 new users)
  4. Questions to Ask Before Your Child's Frenectomy (110 new users)
  5. How Long Do White Crowns for Kids Last? (72 new users)

Four of your five top pages are articles, and two of them are about frenectomies. That pattern is confirmed when you look at performance by topic cluster. Frenectomy Procedures is your highest-performing cluster by a wide margin. Pediatric Dental Crowns is also growing. These two clusters are where your content is doing the most work right now, and they reflect real patient demand that the site is increasingly well-positioned to meet.

Local Listing

Calls from your Google listing came in at 81 this quarter, down 11% Quarter-over-Quarter from 91 last period but up 27% Year-over-Year from 64 a year ago. That Year-over-Year comparison is the more meaningful one here: more patients are picking up the phone after finding your listing than at this time last year, which reflects real listing health.

Website clicks from the listing held at 106, down 5% Quarter-over-Quarter and roughly flat Year-over-Year, while listing views came in at 1,491 - nearly identical to last quarter. The listing is stable, and the Year-over-Year call growth is a positive sign. Adding to that picture, your practice earned 13 new reviews this quarter at a 5.0-star average, which reinforces credibility for anyone who finds your listing while searching for a pediatric dentist.

Key Observations

There is a meaningful disconnect emerging between the top of the funnel and the data (or lack thereof) we're seeing at the bottom with Key Events.

Search clicks and sessions are up sharply, new users are arriving in much larger numbers than a year ago, and your listing is generating more calls Year-over-Year - yet key events on the website have nearly vanished this quarter. When traffic grows and conversions collapse simultaneously, the most likely explanation is a measurement issue rather than a behavior change: a form that stopped firing a confirmation event, a tracking tag that broke, or a configuration update that changed what gets counted.
