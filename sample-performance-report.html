About this data

Every metric here is pulled directly from Google’s own systems — the same data that powers Google Search Console, Google Business Profile, and Google Analytics.

You may notice small differences compared to those dashboards. This is expected and affects every reporting tool — Google withholds certain low-volume search queries from its API for privacy reasons, and date range boundaries can vary slightly between platforms. The trends and directional changes are fully reliable.

Search visibility — Google Search Console
Website traffic — Google Analytics, organic search
Listing activity — Google Business Profile

Q1 2026 delivered exceptional growth across search and website performance, with sessions up 96% and search clicks up 97% Quarter-over-Quarter. Call volume from your Google listing rose 35% Quarter-over-Quarter and 80% Year-over-Year, reflecting sustained momentum in patient acquisition across every channel we track.

Brief Summary

Q1 2026 was a standout quarter across nearly every channel we track. Website visitors from Google and search visibility both roughly doubled compared to the previous quarter, and the Year-over-Year picture is even stronger. The most meaningful signals this period are the surge in search clicks and the sustained growth in new visitors finding your practice for the first time.

Website Traffic

Your website drew 1,536 sessions this quarter, up 96% Quarter-over-Quarter and 236% Year-over-Year. Of those visitors, 1,187 were new users - up 105% Quarter-over-Quarter - which tells us that a large share of the audience arriving at your site had never visited before. That kind of new-visitor growth is a strong sign that your practice is reaching people earlier in their search, before they have already found you through other means. Those visitors completed 57 key events, up 19% Quarter-over-Quarter. The conversion rate against a much larger visitor base holding steady and still growing is a healthy pattern; it means traffic quality came along with traffic volume.

Search Visibility

Search clicks reached 1,258 this quarter, up 97% Quarter-over-Quarter and 414% Year-over-Year. The underlying impression volume climbed to 292,850, up 54% Quarter-over-Quarter - but the click growth outpacing impression growth is the more important part of the story. When clicks grow nearly twice as fast as impressions, it suggests your listings are appearing for searches that are a stronger match for what families are looking for, and that the way your pages appear in results is earning more attention. From a patient acquisition standpoint, 1,258 people actively chose to visit your site from a search result this quarter - nearly double what we saw three months ago.

Local Listing

Your Google listing generated 92 calls this quarter, up 35% Quarter-over-Quarter and 80% Year-over-Year. That is the signal that matters most here - calls represent families who saw your listing and picked up the phone. Website clicks from the listing came in at 115, up just 6% Quarter-over-Quarter, while listing views dipped to 1,494, down 5% Quarter-over-Quarter and 28% Year-over-Year. The view decline is worth watching, though it has not yet affected call volume, which continues to climb. Adding to listing health, you earned 15 new reviews this period at a 5.0-star average - a strong trust signal for any parent researching pediatric dental care.

Key Observations

The most interesting pattern this quarter is the divergence between your Google listing and your website search performance. Listing views are down Year-over-Year while search clicks and website sessions are growing sharply. This suggests that more prospective patients are finding you through organic search results and going directly to your website, rather than interacting with your listing in the map pack. That shift does not indicate a listing problem - calls are still up strongly - but it does mean that your website is doing more of the acquisition work than it was a year ago, and that the investment in search visibility is increasingly the primary driver of new patient interest.
