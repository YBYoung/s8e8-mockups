The ten report-card grades arranged by phase, from intake to refinement. Wires show what feeds what; a failing feed runs red, and everything living off it is flagged before its own numbers ever slip.

How to read it: the ring is the category's existing grade, nothing is recalculated. Migrations sits on an optional leg between Onboarding and Strategy: skipping it is normally fine, the dev team increasingly handles migrations themselves, so a stall there never puts the path in jeopardy. Support runs beside the path, not on it. A dashed card means an upstream feed is failing: the risk is starvation, not lateness.
