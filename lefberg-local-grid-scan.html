Method and how this compares to Local Falcon
Why not Local Falcon
Local Falcon was down on Sep 24, 2026 with these three scans stuck at "Scan In Progress". This scan reproduces the same 9 × 9, 1.0-mile setup by querying Google Maps directly through DataForSEO's live SERP API, one query per grid point per keyword (243 queries, top 20 results each, map zoom 14).
Grid
Centered on the office at 35.4860, -82.5550. Points are 0.25 miles apart, running 1 mile north, south, east and west of the office. All 81 points were scanned; Local Falcon reports 75 data points for the same setup, so its edge handling differs slightly.
Average rank (ARP)
Mean of Lefberg's rank across the points, with a point where they are outside the top 20 counted as 21. Lefberg was in the top 20 at every point, so this equals Local Falcon's ATRP here.
Share of local voice (SoLV)
Share of points where the practice ranks in the top 3, the part of the results a searcher sees without expanding the map.
Position among competitors
Every listing's own average rank was computed from the same 243 SERPs, then sorted. No separate scans from competitor addresses are needed: each SERP already lists every practice at that point.
Local density
Count of distinct practices in the top 20 across the area, excluding Lefberg's two listings. Per keyword: 35, 33 and 42 in the top 20; 5, 5 and 6 ever reach the top 3.
Caveats
Maps results shift day to day and by device, so treat single-point differences of one rank as noise. Google personalises "near me" more heavily than the city keywords. Business categories, review counts and coordinates are as Google returned them on scan day.
