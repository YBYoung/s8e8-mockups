Brief Summary

You are a solid top-3 practice for cosmetic dentistry across the western and central parts of the area, but that strength does not carry over to implants or Invisalign. On implants you sit around 6th to 7th behind a specialist and two general practices, and on Invisalign you never reach the top 10 outside a few points near your office because the orthodontists take those spots.

Key Observations

  • Cosmetic dentistry is your strong keyword. You rank 3rd on average, in the top 3 at 69% of points, and 1st or 2nd in the cells around your office and across the west. Rockville Centre for Dentistry and The Smilist hold spots 1 and 2 almost everywhere; both are about 0.3 miles east on the N Park Ave and Lincoln Ave downtown block and both carry more reviews (477 and 965 against your 239).
  • Dental implants is a specialist's keyword and it shows. Dental Implants and Periodontology of Rockville Centre ranks 1st at every single point, with Rockville Centre for Dentistry 2nd. You average 7.4, reach the top 3 at only 5% of points, and drop to 9 to 11 in the eastern third.
  • Invisalign belongs to the orthodontists. You average 11.9 and never reach the top 3. Rockville Centre for Dentistry and The Smilist still lead, then Dr. Doug, Halberstadt, Palmieri and Bitton Orthodontics fill the next spots. Your best points are 5 to 8, all within a quarter mile of the office.
  • The whole map tilts east. On all three keywords you are strongest west and north of the office and weakest in the eastern third, where a searcher is standing closer to the downtown cluster of practices on N Park Ave, Maple Ave and Sunrise Hwy.
  • Two practices set the bar. Rockville Centre for Dentistry averages 1.7 across all 243 points and The Smilist 2.8. Beating either on a service keyword means matching them on reviews and on dedicated service pages, not just proximity.
