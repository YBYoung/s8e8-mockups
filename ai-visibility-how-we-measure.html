AI Visibility

How we measure

Why a Snapshot takes about an hour

AI tools do not give the same answer twice. Ask ChatGPT the same question a few minutes apart and the practices it names can change. One answer is a coin toss, not a measurement.

So a Snapshot asks every question 5 times on each AI, in 5 rounds that start about 12 minutes apart. The wait is the price of numbers that hold up; the page fills in live and keeps going if it is closed.

One question, asked 5 times over the hour.

What we ask, and where

The questions are written from the practice's website, in the words patients use. The SEO can change the words, leave some out, add new ones, and switch each between naming the city and near me.

Each question goes to ChatGPT, Gemini, Google's AI Overviews, Google's AI Mode and Perplexity, each set to the practice's city where it allows. Near-me questions skip the main ChatGPT line, which can't be set to a city, and go to its near-me line instead.

A question goes out to every AI line.

What counts as named

An answer names the practice when it uses the full name, a safe short form of it, or the website. A practice with a similar name never counts.

Cited means the practice's own website is among the answer's sources. A small AI model also labels each answer that names the practice: recommended, listed among options, or mentioned in passing.

For a gentle cleaning in Bothell, Bothell Family Dentistry gets strong reviews; Bothell Family also takes most insurance. Another option is Bothell Smiles Family Dentistry. Source: bothellfamilydentistry.com
What the reader checks in every answer.

The two numbers for each AI

Every AI gets two numbers, side by side in the table. The first is simply what happened: how many of the answers collected named the practice, for example 73 of 120.

The second is the best estimate of how often the practice really comes up: out of every 100 times a patient asks, about how many answers would name it. A Snapshot only collects a sample, so this is a range, for example 52 to 69. The more answers come in, the tighter the range.

Every 100 times a patient asks.

Rivals and the sites AI reads

Rivals are counted the same way as the practice, in the same answers, so the comparison is fair. Practices AI names that are not on the rivals list show up under Also named by AI.

The sites AI cites are counted once per answer. Google's own search and map links are left out.
