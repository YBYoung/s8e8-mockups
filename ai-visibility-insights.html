Insights

Three things worth doing for Expedition, then the detail behind every number.

1Facts AI gets wrong

AI mixes the practice up with "Expedition Pediatric Dentistry & Orthodontics" in Missouri.

It gives the Bellevue practice the Missouri doctors and braces, which it does not offer: 9 wrong facts in 7 answers. Phone, address and hours were right every time AI gave them. Every wrong fact came from a question that uses the practice's name.

Checked against the client's CRM record and client context (the CRM wins when they disagree). Facts with no data behind them are not flagged; prospects are not checked.

One task for this Snapshot, assigned to the client's SEO, MS following.

Each answer counts a practice once, however many times it repeats the name. Questions that use the practice's name are left out.
