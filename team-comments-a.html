Dental Implants in Lakeside

A missing tooth affects more than your smile. It changes how you chew, how you speak, and over time, the shape of your jaw. At Lakeside Family Dental, we replace missing teeth with dental implants that look, feel, and work like the real thing.

What is a dental implant?

A dental implant is a small titanium post that takes the place of a tooth root. Once it bonds with your jawbone, we attach a custom crown on top. The result is a tooth that stays put when you eat, laugh, and brush.

Why patients choose Lakeside Family Dental

Dr. Patel has placed more than 1,200 implants over 15 years. We plan every case with a 3D scan, so you know exactly what to expect before treatment starts. We also offer same-day implants for patients who qualify.

What recovery looks like

Most patients are back to work the next day. You may feel some soreness for a few days, which over-the-counter pain relief usually handles well. We check in after one week and again once the implant has healed.

Cost and insurance

We accept most PPO plans and offer financing for larger treatment plans. Every consultation includes a written estimate, so there are no surprises.

Book a consultation

Call our Lakeside office or book online. Your first visit includes an exam, a 3D scan, and a clear plan for next steps.
