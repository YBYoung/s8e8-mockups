SEO Intake Form: what the portal needs to rebuild it

Everything required to host the SEO strategy homework inside the client portal and hand the answers back to OpenedQueries: the question list, the answer format, test data to build against, and the data hand-off route.

The short version

The intake form is five sections of questions with a known answer shape. To integrate it, the portal needs the question list (below), the answer format (below, with full test data), and an agreed route for the data to reach OpenedQueries. That route already exists: OpenedQueries syncs the marketing homework from the main S8E8 database every night through the read-only gateway. The SEO homework can travel the exact same road.

One live touchpoint: Section 3 questions are not fixed. They are generated per practice by OpenedQueries based on the services chosen in Section 1. The portal calls one endpoint to fetch them (contract in the Section 3 notes below).

How the form behaves

  • Five sections, filled in order. Section 3 stays locked until Section 1 is complete, because its questions are generated from the Section 1 answers.
  • Autosave. Answers save as the client types; clients can leave and come back. Partial answers are normal: real submissions arrive with some questions blank or missing entirely, so treat every answer as optional at the data level.
  • Lifecycle statuses: sent → in_progress (first answer saved) → submitted. On the OpenedQueries side two more exist that the portal never sets: incorporated (answers folded into strategy work) and skipped.
  • Every text question is free-form long text. Only Section 1 (checklist plus ranking) and the two Section 4 radio questions are structured.
  • Some questions are customizable per client. The agency side can override the default wording of Sections 2, 5, and the differentiator question before sending. If the portal hosts the form, we either drop that feature or the portal reads the overrides too; worth a quick decision, not a blocker.

Section 3 · Your Expertise Dynamic

This section has no fixed questions. After Section 1 is complete, OpenedQueries generates three tailored questions per priority service using AI (for example, a practice that prioritized All-on-4 Implants gets three questions specific to how they handle All-on-4 cases).

Answer keys follow the pattern {service_name}_q{index} with a zero-based index: Dental Implants_q0, Dental Implants_q1, Dental Implants_q2. The generated question text is stored alongside the answers (in ai_questions) so the pairing is never lost, and question_sources records whether each question came straight from the AI ("ai") or was reworded by the team before sending ("edited").

Endpoint contract for the portal

When the client finishes Section 1, the portal calls OpenedQueries with the priority services and gets the generated questions back. Response shape:

{
  "questions": {
    "Dental Implants":  ["question text", "question text", "question text"],
    "Invisalign":       ["question text", "question text", "question text"],
    "Sedation Dentistry": ["question text", "question text", "question text"]
  }
}

Generation takes a few seconds, so the portal should show a loading state. Results are cached: calling again with the same services returns instantly (the response then includes "source": "cached"). If the client goes back and changes their services, the same call regenerates. The current endpoint is tied to our internal form tokens, so we will stand up a portal-authenticated version of it; that is expected work on our side, not a problem.

Agency-side emails

Two client-facing emails belong to this form today. If the portal takes over sending them, here is the exact copy, sender behavior, and timing. (A third thing happens on submission, an internal notification to the S8E8 team plus an Asana update, but that stays on the OpenedQueries side either way.)

Sender identity, both emails

  • Sent from the client's Marketing Strategist: their name as the from-name, their title and headshot in the email header, and Reply-To set to their address.
  • If no strategist is assigned, it falls back to a generic "Studio Eighty Eight" sender with the signature block "Studio Eighty Eight / Dental Marketing & Content Strategy".
  • The assigned SEO is BCC'd on the invite; an optional CC list can be set per client.

Email 1 · The invite

Sent when the team clicks Send. The deadline defaults to 14 days out. The greeting name and the two intro paragraphs are editable per client before sending; everything below is the default copy. Highlighted parts are placeholders filled in at send time.

Subject
{Practice Name} -- Content Strategy Questionnaire
Preheader
Your content strategy questionnaire is ready -- due {Month Day}

Content Strategy Questionnaire

Hi {greeting name},

We have prepared a content strategy questionnaire for {Practice Name}. Your responses help us understand your practice and build a content plan tailored to your goals.

The questionnaire takes about 20 minutes and covers your services, patients, and priorities. You can save your progress and return anytime.

Please complete it by {Month Day}.

Start Questionnaire

"Resend" from the agency side sends this same email again unchanged: the deadline stays put, and the form link's 30-day expiry clock restarts.

Email 2 · Deadline reminders

Automated. A daily job checks every sent or in-progress form and sends a reminder when 50% of the deadline window has elapsed, and again at 80%. Each threshold fires exactly once (the send log makes it idempotent), and archived clients are never nagged. The urgency phrase reads "in the next few days" when 3 or fewer days remain, otherwise "within {N} days".

Subject
Reminder: Your strategy questionnaire is due soon
Preheader
Your strategy questionnaire is due {urgency phrase}

Questionnaire Reminder

Hi {client name},

Your strategy questionnaire for {Strategy Name} is due {urgency phrase}. Your responses help us build a content strategy tailored to your practice.

It only takes about 20 minutes to complete. Pick up right where you left off.

Continue Questionnaire

Copy note that applies to everything client-facing: no em dashes, ever. The double hyphen in the subject lines above is the real, intended character.

Getting the data into OpenedQueries

Option A · Portal writes to the S8E8 database, OpenedQueries reads it Recommended

This is the pattern already running in production for the marketing homework: the portal stores answers in the main S8E8 database, and OpenedQueries pulls them nightly (and on demand) through the read-only database gateway. To extend it, we agree on one new table in the S8E8 database that holds the SEO answers in the shape above, keyed to the client. OpenedQueries requests read access to that table through the gateway and syncs it the same way it already syncs the marketing homework.

  • No new API surface, no credentials exchanged.
  • OpenedQueries stays read-only against the S8E8 database, same as today.
  • The only joint work is agreeing on the table's columns, which this document mostly settles: the client key plus the six fields in the test data (status, selected_services, priority_services, ai_questions, question_sources, section_responses) and a submitted-at timestamp.

Option B · Portal pushes to an OpenedQueries endpoint

OpenedQueries exposes an authenticated endpoint the portal calls when a client submits. Workable, but it means minting and managing an API key between the systems and building submission-retry behavior on the portal side. More moving parts for the same result, so Option A is preferred.

Either way, the portal still needs

  • The Section 3 question-generation endpoint (one call per form, contract above).
  • A way to know which client a form belongs to. The S8E8 client id is the natural key since both systems already share it.
