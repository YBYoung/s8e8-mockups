Schorr Story Model

A plan for Schorr Architects

One place for every project, fee and hour at Schorr.

Billing, budgets and timesheets move out of one spreadsheet per project and into one platform for the whole firm. It all ties to one client database, with a forecast months ahead that shows how solvent the firm is at any time.

Prepared for Nathan, Schorr Architects
Starting the second week of December 2026

What you told us

The spreadsheet works. It just has to do too much.

"So, the spreadsheet works, but it is unwieldly. ... I do this for my projects currently, but it needs to be firm wide."Nathan, answers to our questions

The plan

A proof step, then six stages, built from what you asked for.

About a month per stage, with the first version around July 2027.

Each stage is tested by your team on real work and accepted before it counts. You can stop after any accepted stage and keep everything you've paid for.

How a stage is accepted

"Done" means it passed the checks we agreed, on your real work.

Every stage walks the same route, from checks agreed before work starts to a switch-over on a date you choose.

The overlap period

Your spreadsheet stays the official record for 3 to 6 months.

Invoices keep coming from your current method while the platform runs alongside it.

Changes to the plan

New ideas are welcome, and the plan never grows without you knowing.

Using the platform will give your team ideas. Every request goes on a shared change list and is sorted into one of three kinds.

What we need from your team

A little time, at the right moments.

One decision-maker, a few short set-up tasks, and real use of each stage during its 10-business-day testing window.

Security and your data

Two-factor authentication for everyone.

Staff sign in with their own Microsoft 365 accounts, and each sign-in needs a second step, such as a code or an approval in the Microsoft Authenticator app. A stolen password alone can't get in. The platform refuses a sign-in without it, and every account that runs the platform needs it too.

Ownership and support

You own every account from day one, and the code as each stage is paid.

Software that keeps improving after you start using it.

Today

A tab per project for the budget, the engineers and their fees, and the fee split across seven milestones. Each month you enter percent complete, which tells the Controller what to bill and what each engineer is owed.

What's hard

It's unwieldy, and it covers only your projects. No way to bill hourly. Setting up a job should be quick, and you want to see months ahead.

What you want

Billing, budgets and timesheets tied to one client database, firm-wide; a forecast; profit per job; help finding RFQs worth pursuing.

Stage 5About a month, after stage 4

With Sage 50 (to confirm)

Each month your Controller imports one file holding the issued invoices, new clients and engineer bills. Which invoices have been paid comes back the same way, by file.

If you move to QuickBooks Online

Invoices go across as soon as they're issued, and payments your Controller records in QuickBooks come back automatically, checked at least once a day, so the forecast stays within a day of your books.

Only after it's proven: tested against a practice QuickBooks company, then side by side with your books for at least one month end, and approved by Intuit, the company that makes QuickBooks. Moving your history across is best timed to a fiscal-year start.

Something stops your work? We fix it first, and the 10 days pause. Up to three rounds of fixes are included.

Each stage: 25% when it starts, 75% once accepted, or another schedule you prefer.

Overlap3 to 6 months

At each month end, a comparison shows sheet against platform, project by project and line by line, with every difference explained. If something doesn't match, nobody is billed wrongly; we find out why and fix it.

  • No double entry: each month the platform takes the same percent-complete figures from your sheet, so nobody types them twice
  • You switch over when you choose, in writing, for example after three month ends in a row match to the cent
  • The same idea applies to each stage: budgets against your budget tab, timesheets against however hours are tracked today
