Part 1 · Bothell Family Dentistry · check #23

Read for the US, with a Washington side read

2 topics, 4 phrases, judged on July to September 2026. Dental searches overall dipped a little: 97 searches for every 100 a year earlier.

How to read the words

Dental searches overall: how much people search for dentists in general. Market Trends measures it with 5 common dental searches: invisalign, root canal, wisdom teeth removal, veneers and teeth whitening.

Rising: searches for the topic are growing clearly faster than dental searches overall. Steady: moving about the same as dental searches overall. Fading: clearly slower. Emerging: small but growing fast.

Clearly means more than 15 in every 100 ahead or behind, comparing July to September with the same three months a year earlier. Example: if dental searches overall stay flat and a topic grows 20 in every 100, it is 20 in every 100 ahead, so it is Rising.

What changed
  1. No dive at the end. Live plots October 2026, which is not over, as a full month, so every line drops and the end dot and labels sit on the drop. Now October is dotted and the end marks sit on September.
  2. The readout never moves the chart. Live: a wrapping row that grows to two lines and pushes the plot down on every hover. Now: a fixed 48px bar with one slot per line, idle on September's values instead of "Point at the chart".
  3. Direction has a picture. The approved last 12 / 12 before bands come back, each with its average as a level line, so "Steady, about 9 in every 100 higher than a year ago" is visible.
  4. Washington is the phrase's own colour, dashed. Live uses cyan #0891B2, too close to the teal lead to tell apart. Tooth implant's Washington line is added; tooth whitening's is left out as too thin.
  5. Calmer chrome, approved values. Grid at 0 / 50 / 100 not five lines, 10.5px light axis type with ticks, a 4.0 casing not 5.4, the dark dashed hover guide with a big dot on the lead only, and no legend that repeats the end labels.
  6. 5 years reads the trend. A thin raw month under a bold 3-month average, and switching range zooms the same drawing instead of redrawing it.

Why the live strip was wrong (T6)
Live averages raw levels over 36 months, so the 2025 step-up made later months look busier: it said "Tongue-tie peaks in July" (its busiest months were really May, July and November) and gave Pediatric Dentistry "no season", the one topic with a real one.
Strip fixes
5 fixed teal steps mapped linearly from each topic's quietest to busiest month (live squared the scale, so most cells looked the same), 2px white gaps, 4px corners, a 1.5px ink ring on a repeating peak, three-letter months, numbers on hover.
